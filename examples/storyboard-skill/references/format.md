# Required output format

Use these four sections in this exact order:

creative_summary:
A concise statement of subject, goal, setting, and tone.

visual_direction:
Composition, subject motion, camera behavior, lighting, palette, material, and continuity. Apply every attached image reference explicitly by its zero-based `@image_N` label.

audio_direction:
Diegetic sound, dialogue if requested, ambience, and music direction. If the requested medium has no audio, write `none`.

negative_constraints:
Concrete mistakes to avoid, including identity drift, unrequested text, broken continuity, or conflicting camera instructions.
