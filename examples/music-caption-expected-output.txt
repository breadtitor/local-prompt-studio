### Global Metadata

Instrumental retro-futurist electronic music in steady 4/4 with a measured mid-tempo pulse. The
track moves from nocturnal restraint through focused momentum to a controlled, optimistic peak,
then returns to a reflective after-hours mood. Warm analog bass supplies the foundation while
glassy arpeggios, soft-edged pads, restrained drum-machine hits, and occasional clean guitar
harmonics create a polished neon atmosphere. Keep the mix spacious and dynamic, with clear
transients, gentle stereo movement, and no harsh distortion, festival-scale impact, or crushed
mastering.

### Vocal Details

The piece is fully instrumental with no lead vocal, backing vocal, spoken phrase, or choir. A
glassy synthesizer motif carries the primary melodic role, while clean guitar harmonics answer it
sparingly at transition points. Treat both as expressive but understated voices within the
arrangement, using short ambience and controlled delay rather than conspicuous effects.

### Arrangement

The Intro begins with a distant pad, a filtered pulse, and isolated high arpeggio notes, leaving
wide gaps before the rhythm appears. During Development, introduce the warm bass in short phrases,
then establish a restrained kick, dry snare, and fine closed-hat pattern. Let the arpeggio become
more continuous while subtle guitar harmonics mark every second transition; add motion through
filter opening and small rhythmic variations instead of extra density. At the Climax, widen the
pad, strengthen the bass sustain, and let the full melodic motif resolve over the most complete
drum pattern, but avoid a drop, oversized fill, or aggressive limiting. For the Outro, remove the
snare first, thin the arpeggio, and fade the pad's upper layer. End with the original filtered
pulse and one final synthesizer motif decaying into a quiet stereo field.
